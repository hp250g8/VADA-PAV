About Us

Serving Fresh, Hygienic & Tasty Street Food

Welcome to Hygenic Cart — where great taste meets complete cleanliness.

We are a street-food business built on three pillars: tasty food, strict cleanliness, and customer satisfaction. Every dish is prepared fresh, with ingredients sourced carefully and handled with the utmost hygiene, so you can eat with confidence and enjoy every bite.

Our menu is a celebration of Indian street food — from the beloved Vada Pav to the tangy Dabeli and the rich, buttery Pav Bhaji. We take pride in using only fresh, quality ingredients and following hygienic preparation practices at every step.
